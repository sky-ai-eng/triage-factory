Field reference:
- outcome: how the run ends. One of:
  - "finish" — you accomplished the mission; the task is done and should close. This is normal completion.
  - "abort" — you cannot safely complete the mission (a precondition failed, the situation is ambiguous or unsafe, or it's provably impossible with the tools and context available). The task stays open for a human. Add a "reason" field explaining precisely what blocked you and what a human needs to do to unblock.
- summary: a brief natural-language description of what you did — required on a "finish".
- reason: required on "abort" — the "why I stopped / what a human needs to do" note. Distinct from summary; omit it for non-abort outcomes.

An abort looks like:
{"outcome": "abort", "summary": "<what you did before stopping>", "reason": "<why you stopped / what a human must do>"}

Do NOT include any other text in your final message. Only the JSON object.
